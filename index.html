📊 Stat league table
All teams ranked by a chosen stat, with historical spread. Likely range = average ± 1 std dev (clamped at 0), roughly a 68% band — a rough guide, not a hard prediction. Early season with few games played, ranges will look wide/jumpy — that's genuine small-sample noise, not a bug, and it settles down as more matches come in (rows under 8 games are dimmed with a ⚠️).
